# Backlog

Ideas liked but not built.

- The cloud: a three-dimensional embedding as a turning nebula for the opening.
- Time as depth: composition century on the third axis, to separate convergence from inheritance.
- Each book as a road: a text's verses in reading order drawn as a path across the terrain.
- Which book is this: a guessing game whose confusion matrix is a human-judged overlap measure.
- The long sentence: every verse end to end as one scrubbable ribbon.
- Two voices: two texts and a question as a conversation of real passages.
- Translation shadow: the same text in two translations, to show how much geometry is the translator.
